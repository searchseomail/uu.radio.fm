End-to-End Support

Comprehensive Buyer Services

We guide you through every stage of the Bulgarian real estate purchase with zero legal risks.

Legal Due Diligence

We verify encumbrances, bank mortgages, developer permits (Act 14, 15, 16), and marital rights directly via the Bulgarian Registry Agency (Имотен регистър).

100% Remote Purchase

No need to fly. You can sign a power of attorney at any Bulgarian embassy or with an apostilled local notary. We handle all notary deeds on your behalf.

Residency & Visa D (ВНЖ)

Full immigration support for retirees (pensia program), trade representation (TRO), and investors looking to reside permanently in the European Union.

Bank Account & BULSTAT

Opening local client accounts, VAT registration, BULSTAT tax registrations, and setting up utilities auto-pay (EVN, ViK water, internet).

Turnkey Renovation

English-speaking building contractors. Interior design, furniture packs, air conditioning installation, and repairs with guaranteed deadlines.

Rental Property Management

Booking.com & Airbnb listings, key holding, professional cleaning, check-in/out, and sending profits directly to your bank account worldwide.
